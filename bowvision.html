How it works

The model is a Roboflow-trained object detector. It runs client-side through inferencejs, so frames from your camera never leave the machine — the weights come to you rather than your video going to a server. The bundle is only fetched when you start detection.

  • Bounding boxes are drawn in the source's intrinsic pixel space, then scaled by CSS — which is why they stay aligned at any display size.
  • The confidence slider re-runs inference on a still immediately, so you can watch precision and recall trade off in real time.
  • Camera access is never requested until you press the button, and the stream is stopped the moment you leave the page.
